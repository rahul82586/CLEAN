# MetaTrader 5 Administrator — User Guide

MetaTrader 5 Administrator is a software program for the remote administration of the MetaTrader 5 trading platform. It enables control over servers and other components of the platform, management of user [group](Platform-Setup/Groups.md) settings, [manager](Platform-Setup/Managers.md) configurations, [financial symbol](Platform-Setup/Symbols.md) and [data feed](Platform-Setup/Data-Feeds.md) parameters. Also, the Administrator Terminal allows managing databases of [quotes](Platform-Setup/1-Minute-History-Charts.md) and [tick data](Platform-Setup/BidAskLast-Ticks.md), configuring [trading schedule](Platform-Setup/Time.md) and adjusting [backup](Platform-Setup/Network-cluster/Configuring-Servers/Backup-Server.md) parameters.
