# Trading Operations

The Manager terminal allows you to conduct any trade operations on accounts on behalf of clients: put pending orders, open and close positions, etc. To perform operations, open the Trade tab.

![Conducting trade operations on the account on behalf of the client](images/account_trade.png)

Detailed information about conducting trade operations is available in separate sections:

  * [Opening positions (#open)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Positions.md#open)
  * [Editing positions (#modify)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Positions.md#modify)
  * [Closing positions (#close)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Positions.md#close)
  * [Closing a position by an opposite one (#closeby)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Positions.md#closeby)
  * [Placing pending orders (#place)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Orders.md#place)
  * [Modification and deleting of pending orders (#modify-delete)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Orders.md#modify-delete)
  * [Activation of pending orders (#activate)](../Trading-Operations/Working-with-Trading-Orders.md#activate)



Besides, if you have appropriate permissions, you can [edit orders, trades and positions parameters](../Trading-Operations/Viewing-and-Editing.md) in the trade server databases.
