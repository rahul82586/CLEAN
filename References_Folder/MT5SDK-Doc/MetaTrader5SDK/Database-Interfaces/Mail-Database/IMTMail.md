# IMTMail

The IMTMail class contains the following methods:

Method | Purpose  
---|---  
[Release](IMTMail/Release.md) | Delete the current object.  
[Assign](IMTMail/Assign.md) | Assign a passed object to the current one.  
[Clear](IMTMail/Clear.md) | Clear an object.  
[ID](IMTMail/ID.md) | Get the email ID.  
[Parent](IMTMail/Parent.md) | Get the ID of the parent email.  
[Subject](IMTMail/Subject.md) | Get and set the subject of an email.  
[From](IMTMail/From.md) | Get and set the login of the email sender.  
[FromName](IMTMail/FromName.md) | Get and set the name of the email sender.  
[To](IMTMail/To.md) | Get and set the login of the email recipient.  
[ToName](IMTMail/ToName.md) | Get and set the name of the email recipient.  
[ToRangesAdd](IMTMail/ToRangesAdd.md) | Add a range of logins of email recipients.  
[ToRangesDelete](IMTMail/ToRangesDelete.md) | Delete a range of logins of email recipients by the index.  
[ToRangesClear](IMTMail/ToRangesClear.md) | Clear the ranges of logins of email recipients.  
[ToRangesTotal](IMTMail/ToRangesTotal.md) | Get the number of login ranges of recipients.  
[ToRangesNext](IMTMail/ToRangesNext.md) | Get a range of logins of email recipients by the index.  
[Time](IMTMail/Time.md) | Get the time of email sending.  
[Body](IMTMail/Body.md) | Get and create the email body.  
[BodySize](IMTMail/BodySize.md) | Gets the size of the email body in bytes.  
[AttachmentsAdd](IMTMail/AttachmentsAdd.md) | Attach a file to an email.  
[AttachmentsClear](IMTMail/AttachmentsClear.md) | Clear the list of files attached to an email.  
[AttachmentsTotal](IMTMail/AttachmentsTotal.md) | Get the total number of files attached to an email.  
[AttachmentsBody](IMTMail/AttachmentsBody.md) | Get the attachment body by its position.  
[AttachmentsSize](IMTMail/AttachmentsSize.md) | Get the size of an attachment by its position.  
[AttachmentsName](IMTMail/AttachmentsName.md) | Get the name of an attachment by its position.
