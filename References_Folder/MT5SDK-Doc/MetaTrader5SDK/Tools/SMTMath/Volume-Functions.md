# Volume Functions

The SMTMath class contains the following methods for working with the volumes:

Method | Purpose  
---|---  
[VolumeToInt](Volume-Functions/VolumeToInt.md) | Converting a volume from double to int.  
[VolumeExtToInt](Volume-Functions/VolumeExtToInt.md) | Converting the increased accuracy volume from double to int.  
[VolumeToDouble](Volume-Functions/VolumeToDouble.md) | Converting a volume from int to double.  
[VolumeExtToDouble](Volume-Functions/VolumeExtToDouble.md) | Converting the increased accuracy volume from int to double.  
[VolumeToSize](Volume-Functions/VolumeToSize.md) | Converting the volume from lots to amount.  
[VolumeExtToSize](Volume-Functions/VolumeExtToSize.md) | Converting the increased accuracy volume from lots to amount.  
[VolumeFromSize](Volume-Functions/VolumeFromSize.md) | Obtaining volume in lots from the volume specified as amount.  
[VolumeExtFromSize](Volume-Functions/VolumeExtFromSize.md) | Obtaining extended accuracy volume in lots from the volume specified as amount.
