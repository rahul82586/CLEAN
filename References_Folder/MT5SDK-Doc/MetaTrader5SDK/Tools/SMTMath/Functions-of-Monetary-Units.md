# Functions of Monetary Units

The SMTMath class contains the following methods for working with money:

Method | Purpose  
---|---  
[MoneyAdd](Functions-of-Monetary-Units/MoneyAdd.md) | Add sums of money in the form of two numbers of type double.  
[MoneyEqual](Functions-of-Monetary-Units/MoneyEqual.md) | Compare two quantities of money in the form of numbers of type double.  
[MoneyDigits](Functions-of-Monetary-Units/MoneyDigits.md) | Get the number of decimal places for the specified currency.
