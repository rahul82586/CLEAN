# IMTConSymbolSession

The IMTConSymbolSession class contains the following methods:

Method | Purpose  
---|---  
[Release](IMTConSymbolSession/Release.md) | Delete the current object.  
[Assign](IMTConSymbolSession/Assign.md) | Assign a passed object to the current one.  
[Clear](IMTConSymbolSession/Clear.md) | Clear an object.  
[Open](IMTConSymbolSession/Open.md) | Get and set the opening time of trading or quoting session of a symbol.  
[OpenHours](IMTConSymbolSession/OpenHours.md) | Get the number of hours in the opening time of trading or quoting session of a symbol.  
[OpenMinutes](IMTConSymbolSession/OpenMinutes.md) | Get the number of minutes in the opening time of trading or quoting session of a symbol.  
[Close](IMTConSymbolSession/Close.md) | Get and set the closing time of trading or quoting session of a symbol.  
[CloseHours](IMTConSymbolSession/CloseHours.md) | Get the number of hours in the closing time of trading or quoting session of a symbol.  
[CloseMinutes](IMTConSymbolSession/CloseMinutes.md) | Get the number of minutes in the closing time of trading or quoting session of a symbol.
